When potential customers search for a local business on Google, your business profile can be one of the first things they see.

Whether someone is looking for a plumber, restaurant, contractor, or web designer, your Google Business Profile helps them discover your business, learn what you offer, and decide whether to contact you.

The problem is that many small businesses create a profile and never update it. An incomplete or outdated profile can make your business look less professional and cause potential customers to overlook you.

The good news is that optimizing your Google Business Profile does not have to be complicated.

Here are six ways to improve your profile and help more local customers find your business.

---

## 1. Complete Every Section of Your Profile

One of the easiest ways to improve your Google Business Profile is to make sure your business information is complete and accurate.

Start with the essentials:

- **Business name:** Use your real-world business name, without adding unnecessary keywords.
- **Business category:** Choose the most accurate primary category for your business.
- **Address or service area:** Make sure customers can understand where you operate.
- **Phone number:** Provide a reliable number where customers can reach you.
- **Website:** Link directly to your business website.
- **Business hours:** Keep your regular and holiday hours up to date.
- **Services:** Add the services your business actually offers.

Incomplete profiles can leave customers with unanswered questions. Accurate information makes it easier for people to understand your business and take the next step.

Take a few minutes to review each section and correct anything that is missing or outdated.

---

## 2. Choose the Right Business Categories and Services

Your business category helps Google understand what your business does and which searches may be relevant to it.

For example, a plumbing company should select an appropriate plumbing category rather than a broad category that does not accurately describe its primary services.

Your primary category is especially important, so choose the one that best represents your main business activity.

You can also add relevant secondary categories when they accurately describe other aspects of your business.

Next, review your available services. Add clear descriptions of the work you perform so potential customers can better understand what you offer.

For example, a web design company might list:

- Small business website design
- Mobile-friendly website development
- Website redesigns
- Website maintenance
- Local SEO services, if offered

Only list services you genuinely provide. Adding unrelated categories or services in an attempt to appear in more searches can work against your goal of presenting an accurate business profile.

---

## 3. Add High-Quality Photos and Keep Your Profile Active

Photos give potential customers a better idea of what your business looks like and the quality of your work.

A profile with relevant, genuine images can feel more trustworthy than one with little or no visual information.

Depending on your business, consider adding:

- Your business logo
- A professional cover photo
- Photos of completed projects
- Images of your team at work
- Photos of your storefront or workspace
- Before-and-after project photos

For service-based businesses that travel to customers, photos of completed jobs can be particularly useful.

You should also keep your profile current. When appropriate, share updates about new services, recent projects, seasonal offerings, or important business announcements using Google Business Profile posts.

You do not need to post every day. Focus on useful, accurate information that gives potential customers another reason to choose your business.

Avoid relying entirely on generic stock photos. Authentic images that show your actual work help customers understand what makes your business different.

---

## 4. Get More Genuine Customer Reviews

Customer reviews can influence whether someone decides to contact your business.

When comparing local companies, potential customers often look at ratings, read recent reviews, and pay attention to how the business responds to feedback.

Make collecting reviews a regular part of your customer follow-up process.

Here are a few practical ways to get started:

- Ask satisfied customers to leave an honest review.
- Send customers a direct link to your Google review page.
- Include a review request in your follow-up emails.
- Make it easy for customers to find your review link.
- Respond professionally to both positive and negative feedback.

You should never buy reviews, offer incentives in exchange for reviews, or pressure customers to leave only positive feedback. Reviews should reflect genuine customer experiences and follow Google's review policies.

When responding to reviews, keep your replies professional and personal. Thank customers for positive feedback, and address negative experiences calmly while offering a reasonable way to resolve the issue.

Consistent, genuine reviews help potential customers get a better understanding of what it is like to work with your business.

---

## 5. Optimize Your Website for Local SEO

Your Google Business Profile is only one part of your online presence. Your website should reinforce the same information and help visitors take action once they find you.

Start by making sure your website clearly explains what your business does, which services you offer, and where you operate.

Here are a few ways to improve your local SEO:

- **Create clear service pages:** Give important services their own pages when appropriate.
- **Mention your service areas naturally:** Help visitors understand which locations you serve.
- **Keep business information consistent:** Use accurate business details across your website and online listings.
- **Make your website mobile-friendly:** Ensure visitors can navigate your site easily on phones and tablets.
- **Improve loading speed:** Reduce unnecessary delays that can frustrate visitors.
- **Add clear contact options:** Make it easy to call, email, request a quote, or book an appointment.

For example, a Charleston-based contractor should clearly explain the services it provides and the surrounding areas it serves, rather than relying on visitors to figure it out themselves.

Avoid stuffing city names or keywords into every paragraph. Your content should read naturally and provide useful information to potential customers.

## 6. Track Your Results and Make Improvements

Optimizing your profile is not a one-time task. Reviewing its performance can help you understand how customers discover and interact with your business.

Google Business Profile provides performance data for eligible profiles, including information about how people find your business and the actions they take.

Depending on the data available for your profile, you may be able to review:

- How people discover your business on Google
- Search terms associated with your profile
- Website clicks
- Phone call interactions
- Requests for directions
- Other customer interactions

Use this information to identify opportunities for improvement.

For example, if customers are finding your profile but rarely visiting your website, review your website link, service descriptions, photos, and overall presentation.

If people visit your website but do not contact you, the issue may be your website's design, messaging, or contact process rather than your Google Business Profile itself.

Give changes time to produce meaningful data, and focus on improving the overall customer experience instead of chasing a particular ranking position.

## A Simple Google Business Profile Checklist

If you are not sure where to begin, use this checklist to review your current profile.

- [ ] Verify that your business is claimed and verified.
- [ ] Confirm your business name and primary category are accurate.
- [ ] Update your phone number, website, hours, and service area.
- [ ] Add your relevant services and descriptions.
- [ ] Upload genuine photos of your business and work.
- [ ] Create a simple process for requesting honest customer reviews.
- [ ] Respond to existing reviews.
- [ ] Make sure your website is mobile-friendly and easy to navigate.
- [ ] Review your profile performance regularly.

You do not need to complete everything in one sitting. Start with the missing information, then work through the remaining improvements over time.

## Final Thoughts

Your Google Business Profile can be an important part of your small business's local marketing strategy.

A complete profile, accurate business information, genuine photos, authentic customer reviews, and a well-designed website can help potential customers discover your business and feel more confident reaching out.

However, optimization is not a guarantee of higher rankings or more leads. Local visibility also depends on factors such as relevance, distance, and how well-known your business is.

The goal is to make it as easy as possible for the right customers to find your business, understand what you offer, and choose to work with you.
